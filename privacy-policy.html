Anvil Depths — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Anvil Depths (Android, package com.rustybrichka.anvildepths) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep data to the minimum the game needs.

1. Who we are

Anvil Depths is developed by RustyBrichka Studio, operated by Maksym Kravets, Ukraine. Contact for any privacy question or request: asynsakur@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no contacts, no photos, no precise or approximate location.
  • Anonymous usage statistics help us fix bugs and balance the game. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Usage stats.
  • No ads and no purchases for real money in the current version.
  • We do not sell personal data.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (ore, weapons, gems, upgrades, rewards, achievements), your settings and a random player ID created by the game are saved on your device. While usage stats are on, the game also keeps a local log of the same usage events described in section 4. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account under Google's own terms.

4. Usage statistics

To understand how the game is played, fix bugs and balance difficulty, the game sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider PostHog (PostHog Inc., servers in the United States), which processes them on our behalf.

  • Identifiers: a random player ID and session ID created by the game. They are not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID. The game does not use the advertising ID.
  • Gameplay events: for example forge sessions and timing results, upgrades bought, runs started and finished, depth, rooms cleared, enemies defeated, bosses, deaths, rewards claimed, first-time steps of the tutorial, language changes.
  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, memory size, screen size, game and system language, game version.

Your IP address is necessarily seen by the server when data is sent. We ask PostHog not to derive your location from it and configure our project not to store IP addresses. Usage statistics are not used for advertising.

Your choice: turn off Settings → Usage stats; collection stops immediately and events not yet sent are deleted from your device. Usage data is kept for 12 months and then deleted.

5. Advertising

The current version of Anvil Depths shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. Some test builds show an "Ad (placeholder)" screen for a second and a half before an in-game reward; it is not an ad and collects nothing. If real ads are added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Purchases

The game currently has no purchases for real money. If they are added, payments will be handled by Google Play; we only receive a confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

7. Children

Anvil Depths is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes we have received information about their child can contact us and we will delete it.

8. Sharing

We share data only with the service provider described above (PostHog for usage statistics), or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

9. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our provider may process data in the United States and other countries; it applies contractual and technical safeguards for such transfers.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the usage data linked to your player ID. The beginning of your Player ID is shown in Settings next to the game version; tap it to copy the full ID and include it in your email to asynsakur@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.

12. Contact
