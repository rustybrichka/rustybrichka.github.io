RUN & HATCH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game RUN & HATCH (Android, package com.rustybrichka.runandhatch) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep data to the minimum the game needs.

1. Who we are

RUN & HATCH is developed by RustyBrichka Studio, operated by Maksym Kravets, Ukraine. Contact for any privacy question or request: asynsakur@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no contacts, no photos, no precise location.
  • Players under 13 (and anyone who has not told us their age): no usage analytics, and only child-directed, non-personalised ads.
  • Players 13 and older: anonymous usage statistics that can be turned off at any time in Settings → Share usage data.
  • We do not sell personal data.

3. Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your year of birth. Only the resulting age group (under 13, 13–17, 18 or older) is stored, on your device. Your year of birth is not stored and the age group is not sent to us. It decides which privacy settings apply (sections 5 and 6).

4. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, gems, animals, eggs, rewards, achievements), your settings and age group, and a random player ID created by the game are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account under Google's own terms.

5. Usage analytics (players 13 and older only)

To understand how the game is played, fix bugs and balance difficulty, the game sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider PostHog (PostHog Inc., servers in the United States), which processes them on our behalf.

  • Identifiers: a random player ID and session ID created by the game. They are not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID.
  • Gameplay events: for example runs started and ended, distance, coins, gates answered, crashes, eggs bought and hatched, animals equipped, rewards and offers viewed or claimed, screens opened.
  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, memory, graphics chip, screen size, system language, game version.
  • Performance: frame rate and similar technical measurements.

Your IP address is necessarily seen by the server when data is sent. We configure PostHog not to store IP addresses and not to derive your location from them. Analytics data is not used for advertising.

Your choice: turn off Settings → Share usage data; collection stops immediately and events not yet sent are deleted from your device. Analytics data is kept for 12 months and then deleted.

6. Advertising

The game may show optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example double coins or a revive). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • Players under 13, or whose age is unknown: ad requests are marked as child-directed. No personalised ads and no remarketing; the advertising ID is not used for these requests.
  • Players aged 13–17: ad requests are marked as coming from a teen and, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, as coming from a user under the age of consent, so no personalised ads are shown there.
  • Players 18 and older: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; without consent you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Ad content: we limit ads to family-friendly content ratings (G for players under 13, at most PG for everyone else) and block sensitive ad categories such as gambling, alcohol and dating.
  • To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID and app set ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic information. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • You can change your ad consent from the game's settings when the option is shown, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

7. Children

RUN & HATCH is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: for them usage analytics is off and ads are child-directed. A parent or guardian who believes we have received information about their child can contact us and we will delete it.

8. Purchases

The game currently has no purchases for real money. If they are added, payments will be handled by Google Play; we only receive a confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

9. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers described above (PostHog for analytics, Google for ads), or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

10. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries; they apply contractual and technical safeguards for such transfers.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the analytics data linked to your player ID. Players 13 and older can see their Player ID in Settings; include it in your email to asynsakur@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
