SCOOP & ROLL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game SCOOP & ROLL (Android, package com.rustybrichka.scoopandroll) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep data to the minimum the game needs.

1. Who we are

SCOOP & ROLL is developed by RustyBrichka Studio, operated by Maksym Kravets, Ukraine. Contact for any privacy question or request: asynsakur@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no contacts, no photos, no precise location.
  • The game does not send gameplay data or usage analytics to us. We have no servers that store information about you.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Players under 13, and anyone who has not told us their age, only get child-directed, non-personalised ads.
  • We do not sell personal data.

3. Age screen

On first launch the game asks for your year of birth. Only the resulting age group (under 13, 13–17, 18 or older) is stored, on your device. Your year of birth is not stored and the age group is not sent to us. It decides which advertising settings apply (section 5).

4. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, daily gift), your settings (music, sound, vibration) and your age group are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account under Google's own terms.

5. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of the screen during levels, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a booster, a second chance or double coins).

  • Players under 13, or whose age is unknown: ad requests are marked as child-directed and ad content is limited to the most family-friendly rating. No personalised ads and no remarketing; the advertising ID is not used for these requests.
  • Players aged 13–17: ad requests are marked as coming from a teen, Google applies its protections for teens (no personalised ads) and ad content is limited to ratings suitable for teens.
  • Players 18 and older: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; without consent you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID and app set ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic information. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Where the consent form applies, you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

6. Children

SCOOP & ROLL is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: for them ads are child-directed and non-personalised, and the game itself sends nothing to us. A parent or guardian with a question about their child's privacy can contact us at the address below.

7. Purchases

The game currently has no purchases for real money. If they are added, payments will be handled by Google Play; we will only receive a confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

8. Sharing

The only third party that receives information from the game is Google, to show ads as described in section 5. We do not sell or otherwise share personal data, except when required by law.

9. Security and international transfers

Ad data is sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Google may process data in the United States and other countries and applies contractual and technical safeguards for such transfers.

10. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to export or delete; uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy and your Google and Android settings. If you live in the EU, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. You can always write to us with any question.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
